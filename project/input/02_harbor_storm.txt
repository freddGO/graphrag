After Storm Halden struck Port Avery in October 2023, the Port Avery Harbor Authority hired Northwind Robotics to inspect the damaged harbor. Samuel Okafor, director of the Harbor Authority, said the drones mapped every piling in four days.

The inspection found severe corrosion on the east pier. The Harbor Authority then contracted Ironclad Marine, a construction firm run by CEO Dana Whitfield, to repair the pier. The repair cost later doubled, and Samuel Okafor publicly disputed Ironclad Marine's invoices. Dana Whitfield argued that Northwind's inspection report had underestimated the damage.
