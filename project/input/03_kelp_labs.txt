In 2024 Maya Chen left Northwind Robotics to start Kelp Labs, a startup that monitors kelp farms with low-cost sensors. Tomas Reyes became the sole CEO of Northwind Robotics after her departure.

Kelp Labs partners with the marine science program at Port Avery Community College, which is led by Professor Elena Ruiz. Students in the program test Kelp Labs sensors in the waters near the east pier.

Bluefin Capital also invested in Kelp Labs, again through Priya Nair. Some Northwind shareholders raised concerns about a conflict of interest, since Priya Nair still sits on the Northwind board while funding a company started by its former co-founder.
